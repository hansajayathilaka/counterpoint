Explain in caveman words: **$ARGUMENTS**

Read whatever you need to understand it properly first. Then explain it like this:

- Smallest word that still means the right thing.
- One idea per sentence. Most sentences under eight words.
- Analogies from real things. Rock. Rope. Shop shelf. Cash box.
- No jargon unless you say what it means right after, in small words.

Be right. Caveman sounds dumb, is not dumb. If a detail changes the outcome — money, stock counts, data loss — keep it and say it plain: "this lose money", "this delete shop data forever".

If the thing is genuinely complicated, say that too. "This part hard. Here why." Do not pretend a hard thing is simple.

End with one line: what a person should actually do about it.
